    Licensing & Free Trial¶

    AlphaFloor EFB offers a transparent, pilot-friendly licensing model. You can test every feature without restriction for seven days before deciding to purchase.

    7-Day Free Trial¶

    Every pilot is entitled to a 7-day free trial with full access to all applications and calculations.

    1. Launch X-Plane 12 with any supported Airbus aircraft.
    2. If this is your first time loading the plugin, the license prompt appears on the tablet.
    3. Click Start 7-Day Free Trial.
    4. Your trial period begins immediately. An indicator at the top of the Settings screen displays your remaining trial days.
    5. You do not need to provide a credit card, email address, or account registration to use the trial.

    Purchasing a License¶

    When you are ready to purchase a permanent license:

    • Price: 11.99 € (single one-time payment, lifetime license).
    • In-app Purchase: Purchase the license directly from the EFB tablet within X-Plane 12.
    • Payment Processing: Processed securely through Stripe.
    • Updates Included: All minor and feature updates throughout the 1.x release cycle are included free of charge.

    Activating Your License¶

    After completing your purchase on the website, you will be redirected to an order confirmation screen. Your license is automatically validated in the EFB. You also have two alternative activation methods:

    Method 1: In-App License Key Entry¶

    1. Copy the license key displayed on your confirmation screen (e.g., AFEFB-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX).
    2. In X-Plane 12, open the EFB tablet and tap the Settings icon.
    3. Select the License section.
    4. Paste or enter your key into the License Key field and click Activate.
    5. The status indicator will turn green and display Licensed (Full Version).

    Method 2: Standalone License File (AlphaFloorEFB.lic)¶

    1. On your order confirmation screen, click the Download AlphaFloorEFB.lic button.
    2. Save the file directly into your plugin directory: 
      X-Plane 12/Resources/plugins/AlphaFloorEFB/AlphaFloorEFB.lic
    3. When you load the aircraft, AlphaFloor EFB automatically detects the file and activates your license without requiring manual key entry.

    Multi-Device Policy¶

    • Up to 3 Personal Computers: Your purchase allows concurrent activation on up to three (3) personal computers (e.g., main flight sim desktop, laptop, secondary simulator rig) owned and operated by the same pilot.
    • External Tablets Do Not Consume Activations: iPads, Android tablets, phones, and laptops connected via local Wi-Fi to display the web interface are clients of your main simulator and do not count toward machine activations.

    Offline License Verification¶

    Once your license is verified, AlphaFloor EFB operates completely offline on your local computer.

    Refund Policy¶

    If you encounter technical defects or hardware incompatibilities that cannot be resolved within 14 calendar days of your purchase, contact support at contact@cyberpatate.fr for a prompt, full refund.
