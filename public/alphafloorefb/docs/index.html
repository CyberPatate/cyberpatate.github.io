    AlphaFloor EFB User Guide¶

    Welcome to the AlphaFloor EFB user guide. AlphaFloor EFB is an Electronic Flight Bag designed for Airbus aircraft in X-Plane 12. It provides complete operational flight planning, performance calculations, aircraft weight and balance management, charts, checklists, and ground servicing tools.

    AlphaFloor EFB can be operated directly inside the 3D cockpit tablet or streamed over your local Wi-Fi network to any tablet, iPad, phone, or secondary monitor.

    Supported Fleet¶

    AlphaFloor EFB is optimized for the following aircraft in X-Plane 12:

    • ToLiss A319 (CFM56 and IAE V2500)
    • ToLiss A320 (CFM56 and IAE V2500)
    • ToLiss A320neo (PW1100G and CFM LEAP-1A)
    • ToLiss A321 (CFM56 and IAE V2500)
    • ToLiss A321neo & A321XLR (PW1100G and CFM LEAP-1A)
    • ToLiss A330-900 (Rolls-Royce Trent 7000)
    • ToLiss A340-600 (Rolls-Royce Trent 500)

    All aircraft configurations, fuel capacities, certified structural limits, and dataref interfaces are automatically detected when you load the aircraft.

    Operating Modes¶

    1. 3D Virtual Cockpit Tablet: Operates directly on the pilot tablet screens mounted on the Captain and First Officer window frames. All buttons, tabs, and input fields respond to standard mouse clicks and scroll wheel movements.
    2. External Device (Wi-Fi LAN): Connect your iPad, Android tablet, phone, or secondary computer to the same local network as your simulation PC. Open the local address displayed in the EFB Settings to control the tablet with full touch gestures.
