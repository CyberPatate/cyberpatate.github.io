    Installation¶

    This guide walks you through installing AlphaFloor EFB into X-Plane 12, verifying the setup, and configuring external tablet connections over your local network.

    System Requirements¶

    • Flight Simulator: X-Plane 12 (version 12.00 or later).
    • Operating System:
    • Windows: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit).
    • macOS: macOS Monterey (12.0) or newer (Universal binary natively supporting Apple Silicon and Intel).
    • Linux: Ubuntu 22.04 LTS, Debian 12, Fedora 38 or equivalent 64-bit distribution with standard glibc.
    • Supported Aircraft: Any of the supported ToLiss Airbus models installed in your X-Plane 12/Aircraft/ directory.

    Step-by-Step Installation¶

    1. Download the Release Archive¶

    Download the latest release archive (AlphaFloorEFB-vX.Y.Z.zip) from the CyberPatate Website.

    2. Extract and Move to the Plugins Folder¶

    Extract the downloaded .zip file. You will see a folder named AlphaFloorEFB.

    Move the entire AlphaFloorEFB folder into your X-Plane 12 plugins directory:

    X-Plane 12/Resources/plugins/AlphaFloorEFB/

    Or use plugin management tools such as XLauncher.

    3. Verify Folder Structure¶

    Confirm that the folder hierarchy looks as follows:

    X-Plane 12/
\-- Resources/
    \-- plugins/
        \-- AlphaFloorEFB/
            |-- mac_x64/
            |   \-- AlphaFloorEFB.xpl
            |-- win_x64/
            |   \-- AlphaFloorEFB.xpl
            |-- lin_x64/
            |   \-- AlphaFloorEFB.xpl
            |-- config.json
            |-- user_checklists.json.example
            |-- skunkcrafts_updater.cfg
            \-- user-guide.pdf

    Important: Do not rename the AlphaFloorEFB folder. Keep all files in their respective subdirectories.

    First Launch & Verification¶

    1. Launch X-Plane 12.
    2. From the flight setup menu, load any supported aircraft (e.g., ToLiss A320neo or A321).
    3. Once the 3D cockpit finishes loading, look at the tablet mounted beside the Captain's window.
    4. The tablet screen will power on and display the AlphaFloor EFB home screen.
    5. Click anywhere on the screen with your mouse to interact with the applications.

    Connecting External Devices (iPad, Android, Laptops)¶

    You can use any external tablet, iPad, smartphone, or laptop as a standalone touch EFB while flying.

    1. Connect your external device to the same local Wi-Fi network as your flight simulator PC.
    2. In the 3D cockpit EFB, tap the Settings icon on the bottom navigation bar.
    3. Locate the Remote Tablet Access section. You will see an address such as: 
      http://192.168.1.42:40899
    4. Open your preferred web browser (e.g., Safari on iPad or Chrome on Android/PC) and enter that URL.
    5. The EFB interface will load with full touch functionality. Any action performed on the external device is instantly synchronized with the aircraft and the cockpit screen.

    Tip for iPad users: In Safari, tap the Share button and select Add to Home Screen. This launches the EFB in full-screen mode without browser address bars.

    [!NOTE] EFB remote access through a tablet is not compatible with the ToLiss Display App available with a Pro license.

    Updating AlphaFloor EFB¶

    • Via SkunkCrafts Updater: AlphaFloor EFB includes full SkunkCrafts Updater integration. When an update is published, open your SkunkCrafts Updater client to download and apply the update. Your license file and custom checklists are automatically preserved.
    • Manual Update: Download the new .zip package from the website and overwrite the contents of X-Plane 12/Resources/plugins/AlphaFloorEFB/. Your license file (AlphaFloorEFB.lic or license.json) will remain intact.
